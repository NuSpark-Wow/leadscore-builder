Definitions
Lead score
A 1–10 rating of how ready a lead is for sales. 10 is a perfect fit that is actively buying.
Fit
Is this the right company and the right person? Industry, size, revenue, title, department and more.
Engagement
What has this person done with you? Offers converted, pages viewed, return visits, email replies.
Intent
Is the company showing signs it is ready to buy? Research topics, funding, hiring, leadership changes, website activity.
Weight
How much a category counts toward the final score. Set a weight to 0 to skip that category.
Importance
How much one variable counts inside its category: Low, Med or High. A High variable counts three times as much as a Low one.
Points
The value of each answer, from 0 to 10. 10 is the best possible answer for that variable.
Disqualifier
A deal-breaker, like a competitor or a job seeker. It removes the lead or subtracts points.
Sales
The score reached the sales threshold. The lead goes to the CRM, is assigned to a rep and the rep is alerted.
Nurture
Promising but not ready. The lead gets marketing follow-up until its score rises.
Hold
Too early or too little known. The lead is kept and recycled later.
Fast-track
A lead who asks for a demo, quote or consult goes straight to sales if the company is a good fit, whatever the total score.
Score only what we know
When on, blank variables are skipped instead of counted as zero. Useful when data is still coming in. If fewer than a quarter of the signals are known, the lead goes to nurture instead of sales until more data comes in.

1. Score weights

How much each part counts toward the final 1–10 score. Weights are normalized, so they always add to 100%. Set a slider to 0 to skip that category.

2. Variables

Check the box to use a variable, uncheck it if you can’t track it. Set its importance (Low, Med, High), and open “Edit points” to change what each answer is worth. Use the “This lead” menus to test a lead live.

3. Routing thresholds

Sales: goes to the CRM and a rep is alerted. Nurture: marketing follow-up until the score rises. Hold: below the nurture threshold, kept and recycled later. Any disqualifier routes straight to Disqualified.

A plain summary of this model to set up in the client’s CRM or automation.
